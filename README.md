# What Drives Revenue in India's Game Market?

### A Player Engagement and Platform Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project explores the factors associated with monthly revenue in India's gaming market. It focuses on understanding how player engagement, session duration, gaming platforms, genres, cities and pricing models relate to the revenue generated by different games.

The analysis is performed using Python and its data analysis and visualisation libraries. The goal is to identify revenue patterns, compare game categories and highlight games that may require further attention.

## 2. Business Problem

A mid-sized game-publishing company wants to understand the revenue performance of its games and identify areas where improvements may be needed.

By analysing player activity and game characteristics, the company can gain insights into which factors are associated with higher revenue and which games may need changes in their monetisation strategies or overall performance.

## 3. Project Objectives

The analysis addresses the following four questions:

1. What does `Monthly_Revenue_Lakh` look like overall in terms of its typical value, spread, highest and lowest values?
2. Do `Monthly_Active_Players_K` and `Avg_Session_Minutes` affect `Monthly_Revenue_Lakh`?
3. Does `Monthly_Revenue_Lakh` differ by `Platform`, `Genre`, `City` and `Pricing_Model`?
4. Do games earning below ₹20 lakh in monthly revenue share common platform, genre or pricing-model characteristics?

## 4. Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about games, their monthly revenue, player activity and other characteristics.

Some of the key columns include:

| Column                     | Description                         |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `Game_ID`                  | Unique identifier for each game     |
| `Monthly_Revenue_Lakh`     | Monthly revenue in lakh rupees      |
| `Monthly_Active_Players_K` | Monthly active players in thousands |
| `Avg_Session_Minutes`      | Average session duration in minutes |
| `Platform`                 | Gaming platform                     |
| `Genre`                    | Game genre                          |
| `City`                     | City associated with the game       |
| `Pricing_Model`            | Game pricing or monetisation model  |

## 6. Methodology

The project follows an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) approach.

### Data Inspection and Cleaning

- Inspected the dataset for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent entries and unusual values.
- Identified an extreme value of 1800 in `Avg_Session_Minutes` and replaced it with the median.
- Excluded five games with an `Unknown` genre from genre comparisons while retaining them for other analyses.

### Feature Engineering

Additional analytical columns were created to support comparisons between games:

- `Revenue_per_1000`
- `Engagement_Level`
- `Player_Size`

### Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis uses descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, scatter plots, histograms, box plots and categorical bar charts to investigate the four research questions.

## 7. Key Findings

### Player Engagement and Revenue

- Monthly active players showed a moderate positive correlation with monthly revenue (**0.61**).
- Average session duration showed a weak positive correlation with monthly revenue (**0.17**).
- Player count showed a stronger association with revenue than average session duration in this dataset.

*Note: Correlation indicates association and does not establish causation.*

### Revenue Across Categories

Average monthly revenue was compared across platforms, genres, cities and pricing models.

The observed category averages were relatively similar, with no obvious extreme outliers or dramatic differences.

### At-Risk Games

Games generating less than ₹20 lakh in monthly revenue were classified as **At Risk**.

A total of **30 games** met this condition.

**Platform distribution:**

| Platform | At-Risk Games |
| -------- | ------------: |
| Mobile   |            20 |
| PC       |             5 |
| Console  |             5 |

**Genre distribution:**

| Genre    | At-Risk Games |
| -------- | ------------: |
| Puzzle   |             8 |
| Strategy |             8 |
| Action   |             6 |
| RPG      |             5 |
| Sports   |             3 |

*Unknown genre entries were excluded from this comparison.*

**Pricing model distribution:**

| Pricing Model    | At-Risk Games |
| ---------------- | ------------: |
| Free_With_Ads    |            18 |
| In_App_Purchases |             9 |
| Paid             |             3 |

Mobile games and games using the Free_With_Ads model formed the largest groups among the At-Risk games.

These counts describe the composition of the At-Risk group and do not independently establish that a category has a higher risk rate.


## 8. Conclusion

The analysis suggests that monthly active player count has a stronger positive association with game revenue than average session duration in the dataset.

Revenue across the examined platforms, genres, cities and pricing models was relatively similar. The At-Risk analysis identified 30 games earning below ₹20 lakh per month, with Mobile and Free_With_Ads games forming the largest groups.

These findings can help guide further investigation into game performance, player acquisition and monetisation strategies.

## 9. Limitations

- The analysis is based on the available dataset and may not represent the entire Indian gaming market.
- Correlation does not establish a cause-and-effect relationship.
- The At-Risk classification uses a fixed monthly revenue threshold of ₹20 lakh.
- Category counts should be interpreted alongside the total number of games in each category before drawing conclusions about relative risk.

## 10. Future Improvements

- Investigate additional factors that may influence game revenue.
- Compare revenue against player acquisition and retention metrics.
- Explore trends over multiple months rather than relying only on monthly summaries.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for easier exploration of the findings.
